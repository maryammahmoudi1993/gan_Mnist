## Results (from the executed notebook; digit GAN seed 42, ring toy seeds 0 to 7)

- Digit GAN: after about step 2,500 the loss curves are flat (detective's loss 0.667 and 0.661, accuracy about 60%) while the pictures keep changing.
- Ruler: real digits give confidence 0.96; generated pictures 0.54 (step 0, all read as an "8"), 0.81 (step 1000, peak), 0.75 (step 5000); 10 of 10 digits found at steps 2000 and 5000 but unevenly (57 of 256 read as a 9 and 12 as a 4 at step 5000). No collapse to a few digits, by this crude ruler.
- Ring toy (6,000 steps): clouds covered per seed 8, 5, 4, 4, 4, 6, 8, 7 (mean 5.75); all 8 in 2 of 8 seeds. The generator draws a continuous curve through the clouds (22% to 38% of the points are far from every cloud). The number of covered clouds went down in 20 of 88 consecutive measurements; seeds 2 and 7 peaked and then fell back.

## Doubts / open points

- **A first execution gave slightly different numbers and ran about three times faster.** The notebook imported Keras before `src.gan`, so the setting that switches off oneDNN (set in `src/gan.py`) came too late. The setup cell was fixed and all numbers and texts in this PR come from the fixed setting; the later executions agree with each other. The deterministic setting costs speed (about 0.1 s per step on four CPU cores).
- Mode collapse is measured on the ring toy, not on the digit GAN, whose ruler is crude (it can be confident about pictures that are not digits).
- 5,000 steps (about five passes) are far fewer than the 23,400 steps of the original script.
- The remedies (Wasserstein loss, balancing, minibatch discrimination, conditional GANs) are listed for further reading and were not tested.
- MNIST licence (CC BY-SA 3.0) was read from the documentation inside the Keras package; the authors' website was not reachable.
